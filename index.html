Tretinoin

A topical retinoid used in the treatment of acne and other skin concerns under medical guidance.

Medical supervision required
Prescription medicine in many countries. Can cause irritation and increased sun sensitivity. Suitability, strength and use should be assessed by a qualified clinician. Avoid during pregnancy unless specifically advised by a clinician.
02 / SKIN
CALM + CLARIFY
SKINCARE ACTIVE

Azelaic Acid

An ingredient used in skincare for blemish-prone skin and the appearance of uneven tone.

Cosmetic & medical strengths
Available in different formulations and strengths. Higher-strength preparations may be regulated as medicines. Introduce carefully and seek professional advice if irritation persists.
03 / HAIR
SCALP CARE
HAIR-LOSS TREATMENT

Minoxidil

A topical medicine used for certain types of hair loss. Results and suitability vary.

Check local medicine guidance
Not suitable for every cause of hair loss. Follow the locally approved product information and ask a pharmacist or clinician before use, especially with scalp conditions or cardiovascular concerns.
04 / SKIN
DAILY SERUM
SKINCARE ACTIVE

Niacinamide

A form of vitamin B3 commonly used to support the skin barrier and improve the look of uneven tone.

Everyday skincare
Often found in leave-on serums and moisturisers. Concentrations vary; a higher percentage is not automatically better for every skin type.

Adapalene

A topical retinoid used for acne in suitable patients, depending on local availability and regulation.

Check local availability
May cause dryness, redness or irritation. Follow the product leaflet and seek advice about combining it with other active ingredients.
